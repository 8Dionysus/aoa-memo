# AGENTS.md

## Applies to

Everything under `mechanics/` until a nearer card narrows the lane.

## Role

`mechanics/` is the home for memo-side mechanics: repeatable memory-layer
operations with inputs, outputs, owner splits, stop-lines, validation, and
provenance. It is not constitutional law, proof, runtime, role authority,
routing implementation, KAG truth, private memory, or playbook meaning.

## Shared package law

Each mechanic package has `AGENTS.md`, `README.md`, `DIRECTION.md`, `PARTS.md`,
`OWNER_MAP.md`, `PROVENANCE.md`, `LANDING_LOG.md`, `ROADMAP.md`, `docs/`, and
`parts/` with its index. Its README is the mechanic card and names operation,
trigger, memo-owned inputs/outputs, stronger-owner split, stop-line,
validation, and next route. A package names an operation, not only a topic.

The package card owns the package identity. Its `docs/` card owns active
mechanic doctrine and source docs; its `parts/` card owns functioning rows and
part-local contracts; its `PROVENANCE.md` and retirement decision preserve former
placement only.
Generated companions summarize source maps and are rebuilt from source.

## Shared route law

Use the shortest route that matches the touched semantics. `README.md` is
conditional human/public/topology orientation only. Read `DIRECTION.md`,
`PARTS.md`, `OWNER_MAP.md`, or `PROVENANCE.md` only when the changed semantics
need that surface. A source-doc or functioning-part task follows its nearest
card and target source; historical recovery follows PROVENANCE.md and the
retirement decision.

The four local cards carry only deltas from this law:

- package cards retain mechanic identity, owner boundaries, package-specific
  source families, and closeout concerns;
- `docs/` cards retain active doctrine boundaries and local source families;
- `parts/` cards retain active row and artifact ownership, with each row
  materialized by `README.md`, `CONTRACT.md`, and `VALIDATION.md`;
- `PROVENANCE.md` retains placement history and provenance stop-lines, never
  active law or aliases.

An active part is an operation node, not a legacy index, source-doc dump, proof
verdict, runtime worker, route dispatcher, role policy, KAG substrate truth, or
owner-acceptance receipt. Stronger claims route through the package owner map.

## Boundaries

Active mechanic docs live under `mechanics/<slug>/docs/`; old flat docs paths
stay out of active references. `mechanics/ARTIFACT_TOPOLOGY.md` governs root
technical artifact placement. If a change becomes runtime, proof, role
authority, route implementation, KAG substrate, playbook choreography, or
source doctrine, hand it to the stronger owner.

## Validation ownership

Use the nearest `VALIDATION.md` route after the touched surface is known;
reusable lanes remain in `config/validation_lanes.json`. Package, part,
owner-map, landing-log, artifact, and topology checks remain owned by their
nearest mechanic route; generated mesh companions are rebuilt from source.

## Closeout

Report changed mechanic packages, active docs, parts, owner maps, provenance,
generated companions, and validators, plus any old flat docs-root reference.
