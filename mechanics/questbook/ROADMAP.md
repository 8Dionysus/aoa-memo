# Questbook Roadmap

## Current

Keep memo quest sources lane-first, public-safe, and generated from source.
Keep root-published quest generated views governed by
`parts/generated-views/` rather than moving public read models into
package-local `generated/`.

## When Time Comes

If repeated quest follow-through needs richer lifecycle or relations, grow the
Questbook source contract in this package before adding new root quest shapes.
Route proof, playbook, runtime, role, and source-owner acceptance to their
owners rather than expanding memo quest authority.
