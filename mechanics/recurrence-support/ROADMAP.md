  catalog outputs, mechanics index, AGENTS mesh, and memo validators.
- Add stricter machine checks for witness trace mapping, return-pack refs, and

  `state_capsule`, `decision`, `episode`, `audit_event`, `anchor`, and

  into this package unless their ownership changes. Checkpoint artifacts route
  through `mechanics/checkpoint/`.
- Do not absorb route dispatch, runtime retry policy, actor rights, proof, or
  playbook choreography into this package.
- Keep `PATTERN_LINEAGE_MEMORY.md` outside this package; lineage harvest now
  has the separate `mechanics/lineage-harvest/` owner route.
