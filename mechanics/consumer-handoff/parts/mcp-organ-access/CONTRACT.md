# MCP organ access Contract

## Contract

publishes exact durable-read and candidate-preparation capability ceilings for stack runtime binding

The two owner-bounded contours do not turn MCP into memory authority.

## Durable read

`durable-memory-read` may return only reviewed corpus memory and its explicit
provenance, lifecycle, temporal, current-recall, and stronger-owner posture.
It may return silence, stale, superseded, retracted, or quarantined states; it
must not silently promote them to current truth. Known object resources remain
derived access projections over reviewed corpus objects.

## Candidate preparation

`memory-candidate-prepare` may create one allowlisted repo-local candidate,
prepare a candidate-only reviewed-intake packet, and prepare a forwarding-check
receipt. Every result must preserve evidence refs, provenance, lifecycle,
`direct_durable_write=false`, and the next owner route.

## Stop-lines

Neither contour may accept durable memory, mutate `memo/objects/`, infer proof
or owner acceptance, invoke another MCP, authorize an effect, or widen its
filesystem roots from discovery. Read and candidate credentials and process
catalogs remain disjoint. MCP annotations are descriptive and never security
enforcement.

`config/organ-access.v1.json` owns the exact capability, credential-class,
primitive, effect, idempotency, and rollback identities. `abyss-stack` owns
runtime binding and deployment. `aoa-sdk` owns admission and orchestration.
