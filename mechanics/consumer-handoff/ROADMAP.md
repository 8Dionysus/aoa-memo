# Consumer Handoff Roadmap

## Next

- Keep the six active handoff docs aligned with generated registries,
  examples, schemas, quests, and validators.
- Keep the `aoa-stats` boundary visible as read-only derived movement summary
  posture until repeated stats use proves that a typed handoff example is
  needed.
- Add typed consumer handoff examples only where a downstream owner repeatedly
  needs the same machine-readable contract.
- Keep KAG export activation separate from source-owned publish readiness.

## Not Yet

- Do not turn consumer handoff into a mirror of `aoa-agents`,
  `aoa-playbooks`, `aoa-evals`, `aoa-kag`, `aoa-sdk`, `Tree-of-Sophia`, or
  `abyss-stack`.
- Do not add a generic neighbor-seams bucket; every added doc must name the
  consumer operation and stronger owner split.
- Do not move recurrence, witness trace, service revision, or operational gate
  surfaces here unless they are proven to be consumer-handoff operations rather
  than writeback, retention, or operational-boundary mechanics.
