# Retention Mechanic Legacy

Legacy is the provenance district for historic placement and raw source snapshots behind the active memo mechanic. It is not active law and must not become a place to hide new work.

## Layout

- `raw/`: git-history snapshots mirrored under their former repo-relative paths.
- `INDEX.md`: former path, restored raw snapshot, active route, and source revision.
- `DISTILLATION_LOG.md`: dated accounting for the raw-to-active restoration.

## Use

Operational rules for this legacy lane live in [AGENTS](AGENTS.md). Use `INDEX.md` for the route from historical snapshot to active surface.
