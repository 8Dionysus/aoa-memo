# Retention Direction

The retention mechanic keeps retention review evidence public-safe,
source-linked, and weaker than runtime execution.

Current direction:

- preserve retention markers and watches as reviewable memo surfaces
- keep pre-authorized mechanical maintenance exact, version-compared,
  idempotent, recoverable, and weaker than a durable worker
- keep semantic lifecycle changes proposal-only and bounded by the sole
  operator's attention budget
- require privacy erasure to close ER0-ER9 through exact owner receipts,
  positive controls, negative recovery, and race/rebuild probes
- block private-memory deployment while any residue, exception, missing
  surface, or unresolved model-unlearning obligation remains
- route execution and schedules to runtime owners
- keep private traces out of this public repo
- route proof of retention behavior to `aoa-evals`

Do not let retention wording imply hidden scheduler authority or private data
retention.
