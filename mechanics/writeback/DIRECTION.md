# Writeback Direction

The writeback mechanic keeps memory-layer writeback explicit, source-linked,
candidate-first, and weaker than runtime truth.

Current direction:

- keep generated writeback companions reproducible from source docs and builders
- preserve manual-first and candidate-only language
- route live execution and storage to `abyss-stack`
- route owner acceptance to the owning repository
- keep writeback temperature separate from proof or promotion

Do not let writeback wording imply a live ledger or hidden worker.
